Every mapped transmission tower in Texas

359,632 transmission towers in Texas, as mapped in OpenStreetMap. Zoom in to see each one as a 3D model.

Inspired by Jorge Luis Borges, “On Exactitude in Science.”

About this map

Tower locations are from OpenStreetMap contributors, November 2026. Poles are left out.

Tower shapes and heights are illustrative, based on mapped tags and line voltage.
