Today: operating generators and batteries in ERCOT from the EIA-860M, September 2026, by nameplate capacity. Queue: active projects in ERCOT's Generator Interconnection Status report, October 2026: the 1,766 active projects it lists by name (its summary counts 1,847 requests and 461.4 GW, including some not listed). Of the 114 approved to synchronize by September 30, 28 still listed as planned by EIA stay in the queue; 44 matched to operating EIA plants and 42 more (6.4 GW) that couldn't be matched with confidence are assumed to be operating, and are left out. Queue capacity is as ERCOT reports it, which for repowered plants is the net change, so the all-built total is approximate. ERCOT doesn't publish project sites: each is placed at its interconnection substation when that can be matched by name, at the midpoint of the line it taps, or otherwise at its county's center, with projects at the same spot combined.
