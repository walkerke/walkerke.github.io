What the map challenge added to mapgl

mapgl is the R package this month's maps are built with. When a map needed something the package could not do, the package was changed instead of the map. Fifteen changes landed during the challenge. Thirteen grew out of work on the daily maps. All 15 are in mapgl's development version. Each map below is live.

#30DayMapChallenge Day 27: New tool

Donut clusters sized by what they add up to

A cluster's size follows the total it holds, here megawatts, not the number of points inside it.

cluster_options(donut_weight = "mw") From Day 4: Clusters

Zoom in and the rings split into plants.

3D models at points

Draw a 3D model at every point, sized from the data. Each turbine here is scaled to its own hub height and rotor.

add_model() · add_model_layer() From Day 5: Sight

Zoom and turn the view to walk through the wind farm.

A different shape for each legend row

One legend can mix shapes. The first patch here is a real 10-minute drive area, the second a circle.

patch_shape = list(drive_area, "circle") From Day 7: 10 minute map

Look at the legend.

Hold a legend row to show it alone

Press and hold a row to show that category alone, and hold it again to bring the rest back. A click still hides one row.

add_legend(interactive = TRUE, layer_id = "job-dots", only_button = TRUE) From Day 9: Urban-rural

Hold "Construction", or use the "only" button beside a row.

Tooltips that leave on their own

A tooltip can clear itself after a set time, which also removes one left behind by a tap on a phone.

tooltip_style(hide_after = 3) Asked for in mapgl issue 194

Rest on a well and hold still for three seconds.

Flow map arrows can show any column

An arrow's tooltip can use extra columns of the data. The last line here is one of them.

add_flowmap(tooltip = list(flow = ...)) From Day 24: Network

Hover an arrow. Zoom in and the labels appear.

Also changed

  • Tile files by relative path on Mapbox maps. A PMTiles file that sits beside the page loads on Mapbox maps, and compare maps keep their extra source options. map |> add_pmtiles_source("wells", url = "tiles/data.pmtiles") From Day 6: Vintage
  • Smaller saved maps. A saved map carries only the libraries it uses. The saved maps for Days 6 to 8 each shed about 10 MB. save_html() Landed November 8
  • Legends that leave the logo alone. Legends in the bottom corners sit above the map's logo and credit line instead of covering them. position = "bottom-left" From Day 7: 10 minute map and Day 8: Utopia
  • Hollow legend shapes. A custom legend shape can be an outline, such as a ring. patch_shape = "<circle ... fill='none' />" From Day 8: Utopia
  • Terrain on saved maps. Terrain added without a stated maximum zoom now carries its elevation on saved maps. add_raster_dem_source() From Day 5: Sight
  • GeoJSON files by relative path. A GeoJSON file that sits beside the page can be a source, so large data no longer has to be written into the page. map |> add_source("lines", data = "data/lines.geojson") From Day 7: 10 minute map
  • Every model drawn. Mapbox thins 3D models where they crowd. This turns off density-based thinning. model_allow_density_reduction = FALSE From Day 14: Borgesian map
  • Legend rows that stay put. In a legend at the bottom of the map, the rows no longer shift when the "Reset Filter" button appears. add_legend(interactive = TRUE) From Day 18: NULL
  • Flow map labels that wait for a zoom. Place labels on a flow map can stay hidden until the map is zoomed in far enough to read them. flow_location_labels_min_zoom = 4 From Day 24: Network

About this page

"In the development version" means the change is merged into mapgl on GitHub. None of these changes is in the CRAN release yet.

Mapbox GL JS, the library under mapgl's Mapbox maps, was also updated to v3.34.2 during the month.

The maps use small pieces of public data from the days named: power plants from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (Form 860M); turbines from the U.S. Wind Turbine Database; fast chargers from the Alternative Fuels Data Center; jobs from the Census Bureau's LODES data; orphan wells from the Railroad Commission of Texas; interchange between grid operators from the Energy Information Administration (Form 930).

To move a map, drag it. To zoom, use its buttons, pinch, or hold Ctrl (Command on a Mac) while scrolling.
